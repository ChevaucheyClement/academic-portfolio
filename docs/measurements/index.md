# Electrical Measurements

**Seven laboratory reports · Universidad Nacional de Santiago del Estero · 2025**

I wrote the reports, performed their calculations and drew their conclusions. The laboratory measurements and instrument work were generally carried out with classmates under the supervision of the teaching engineers. All seven original reports are in Spanish.

## Reports

- **TP1 · Ammeter accuracy class.** Compared an analog ammeter with a class-0.5 reference and estimated its class from the recorded indication errors. The published readings concern the ammeter.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp1-ammeter-class.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp1-ammeter-class.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>

- **TP2 · Voltmeter–ammeter resistance methods.** Compared short and long connections and calculated how meter loading affects the resistance estimate.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp2-volt-ampere.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp2-volt-ampere.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>

- **TP3 · Resistance by comparison and substitution.** Applied two separate DC measurement methods and calculated results and uncertainties for each.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp3-comparison-substitution.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp3-comparison-substitution.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>

- **TP4 · Wheatstone bridge.** Balanced a DC bridge to estimate resistance and compare it with a nominal value.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp4-wheatstone-bridge.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp4-wheatstone-bridge.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>

- **TP5 · Three-voltmeter and three-ammeter methods.** Used both AC measurement methods to estimate an RL impedance and evaluate uncertainty.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp5-three-meters.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp5-three-meters.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>

- **TP6 · Single-phase power with a wattmeter.** Measured power with a wattmeter and current transformer, then examined apparent power, power factor and instrument-loading corrections.<br>
  [Read PDF]({{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp6-single-phase-power.pdf' | relative_url }}) · <a href="{{ '/assets/reports/measurements/tp6-single-phase-power.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download PDF</a>
