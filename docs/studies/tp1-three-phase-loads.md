## My contribution and lab setting

I wrote the submitted report, performed its calculations and drew its conclusions. The measurements and instrument work were generally carried out with classmates under the supervision of the teaching engineers.

## Task and method

We observed how resistive, inductive and capacitive loads affected the current phasors of a three-phase network. The lab used resistive and capacitive load banks, a motor, three current transformers and a Fluke 434 power-quality analyzer. The recorded bench conditions were approximately 400 V line-to-line, 230 V phase-to-neutral and 50 Hz.

## Scope and limits

The report documents these observations with analyzer screens and photographs, without a complete numerical table of phase currents and angles. This is a qualitative laboratory finding.
