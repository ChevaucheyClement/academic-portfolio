# TP2 · Insulation-resistance tests

**Electrical Measurements II · Universidad Nacional de Santiago del Estero · 2026**

## My contribution and lab setting

I wrote the submitted report, performed its calculations and drew its conclusions. The measurements and instrument work were generally carried out with classmates under the supervision of the teaching engineers.

## Task and method

We measured insulation resistance on a 0.6/1 kV cable, a 13.2 kV dry transformer and a motor. The lab used a megohmmeter at 1 kV DC for the cable, 5 kV DC for the transformer and 500 V DC for the motor. I calculated time-based resistance ratios from selected readings.

## Findings from the report

| Test object | Recorded readings | Time ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable | 114 GΩ at 30 s; 116 GΩ at 60 s | 60 s / 30 s ≈ 1.02 |
| Dry transformer | 749 MΩ at 1 min; 1.08 GΩ at 10 min | 10 min / 1 min ≈ 1.44 |
| Motor | 352 MΩ at 1 min; 356 MΩ at 10 min | 10 min / 1 min ≈ 1.01 |
