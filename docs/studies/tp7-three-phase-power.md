## My contribution and lab setting

I wrote the submitted report, performed its calculations and drew its conclusions. The measurements and instrument work were generally carried out with classmates under the supervision of the teaching engineers.

## Task and method

We measured active power in a balanced three-wire system using two analog wattmeters connected through current transformers. The report records readings with resistive, inductive and capacitive load arrangements. I used the current-transformer ratio to calculate indicated power from the two wattmeter readings and calculated a separate estimate after instrument-loading correction.

## Scope and limits

The report's conclusion uses the indicated figure. Its simplified uncertainty calculation is about 2.9% and omits the loading correction term, so the two power figures should remain distinct.
